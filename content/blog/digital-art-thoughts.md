The process of creating digital art is both similar to and distinct from traditional art forms. 
While we still rely on fundamental artistic principles, the digital medium offers unique opportunities 
and challenges.

In this reflection, I share my thoughts on the philosophy behind digital art creation and how it 
continues to evolve as technology advances.

## The Digital Canvas

The digital canvas is both liberating and challenging. Unlike traditional media, we have the ability to:
- Undo and redo without consequence
- Experiment with infinite variations
- Layer and composite with ease
- Work non-destructively

## The Human Element

Despite the digital nature of the work, the human element remains crucial. Our artistic decisions, 
emotional responses, and creative vision still drive the process. The tools may be digital, but 
the art remains deeply human.
