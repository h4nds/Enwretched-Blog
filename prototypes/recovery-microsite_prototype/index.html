The Story Behind Recovery

This piece came from long walks in untouched natural spaces — mossy corners and misty springs that felt like portals. The photograph was pushed in post to blend texture, softened focus, and layered mist to create a dreamlike, slightly surreal look.

The name “Recovery” came later: it felt like that quiet moment after chaos, a hidden place that feels like yours for a moment. Technically, it meant exploring new ways of blending photography and digital manipulation while keeping an organic feel.

This prototype is layout-only and intentionally static — no backend or data collection.
